About Me

14 years turning complex systems into reliable software

I'm a backend software engineer based in Tehran, Iran, with 14 years of experience across banking, payments, and network software. For the past 7+ years at TOSAN, Iran's largest payment service provider, my core focus has been designing and maintaining Java backend subsystems for the bank's switch back office.

Day to day, my stack centers on the Java ecosystem: Spring, Spring Boot, and JPA. I've built and maintained several Java subsystems with numerous internal service calls, working continuously with JPA entity & datasource management, complex joins, and Oracle databases, alongside Java concurrency and multi‑threading and security‑aware backend design.

Earlier in my career — roughly 6–7 years ago — I was deeply involved in ATM programming and C/C++ development on the WOSA/XFS standard using the Qt framework. That expertise is still solid, though today it plays a smaller role than my Java/Spring work. I also have working familiarity with Kafka & event‑driven architectures and classical machine learning. Outside of work, I've been learning modern JS full‑stack development through a self‑taught side project with Node.js, Next.js, and Prisma.
