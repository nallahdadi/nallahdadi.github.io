About Me

14 years turning complex systems into reliable software

I'm a backend software engineer based in Tehran, Iran, with 14 years of experience across banking, payments, and network software. Since 2017 I've been part of TOSAN, Iran's largest payment service provider, where I design and maintain mission‑critical ATM and transaction‑processing software built on WOSA/XFS and ISO 8583.

My core strength is Java backend development — building Spring Boot microservices, Kafka‑based event‑driven pipelines, and containerized services with Docker & Kubernetes. Alongside that, I bring a solid systems background in C/C++ and Qt from years of low‑level device and transaction software.

More recently, I've been expanding into applied AI: building LLM / GenAI‑powered features, automating internal workflows with n8n, and applying classical machine learning to real business problems — bringing modern intelligence to traditional financial systems.
